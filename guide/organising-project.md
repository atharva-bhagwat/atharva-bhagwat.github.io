Real projects live in folders on your disk. Analyses that last are organised in a way anyone can understand. Not fancy, just predictable.</p><pre class="q-tree"><code>pybio-project/
├── README.md              what this is and how to run it

Your code reads from <code>data/raw</code> and saves new tables in <code>data/derived</code>.</li><li><strong>Everything in <code>results/</code> can be deleted and rebuilt</strong> by running your code.
