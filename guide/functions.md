Next week you may want to score a different table, or ignore rows with too few reads. If you copy and paste the code, the copies drift apart and you forget which one you trust.</p><aside class="q-callout is-why"><strong class="q-callout-title">The rule of two</strong><p>The second time you copy a block of code, turn it into a <strong>function</strong>. A function is a named recipe. Fix a mistake in one place and everything that uses it gets the fix.</p></aside><h3>A tiny function</h3><div class="qcode"><div class="qcode-bar"><span class="qcode-name">run this</span><button class="qcopy" type="button">Copy</button></div><pre><code><span class="k">def</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nf">double</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">number</span><span class="p">):</span>

<span class="nb">print</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">double</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="mi">4</span><span class="p">))</span>
